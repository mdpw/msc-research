**Q: You compared costs against cloud alternatives — did you actually use those services?**

No — live billing wasn't feasible for a research prototype. The comparison uses Google's published pricing at USD 0.006 per 15 seconds (Google Cloud, 2024) with a transparent usage model. This is a standard approach for cost-effectiveness analysis in systems research where live benchmarking against commercial platforms isn't practical.
