# Appendix C – Guest Survey: Instrument and Results Summary

---

## C.1 Survey Questionnaire

**Title:** Guest Experience with Hotel Room Service Requests

**Purpose:** This survey was conducted to understand how hotel guests currently make room service requests, their attitudes towards voice-assisted alternatives, and their privacy expectations. Responses were used to inform the functional and non-functional requirements of the system described in this report.

**Eligibility criterion:** Respondents must have stayed in at least one hotel in the past 12 months.

**Distribution method:** Convenience sampling — administered in person and via messaging to family members, relatives, and colleagues known to the researcher.

---

### Section A — About Your Hotel Experience

**Q1.** How many times have you stayed in a hotel in the past 12 months?
- [ ] Once
- [ ] 2–3 times
- [ ] 4 or more times

**Q2.** When you are staying in a hotel and need something (e.g., extra towels, food, housekeeping), how do you usually make that request?
- [ ] Call the front desk by phone
- [ ] Go to the front desk in person
- [ ] Use a hotel app on my phone
- [ ] Ask hotel staff I see in the corridor
- [ ] Other: _______________

**Q3.** Have you ever experienced a delay or miscommunication when making a service request during a hotel stay (e.g., the wrong item delivered, request forgotten, phone not answered)?
- [ ] Yes
- [ ] No
- [ ] Not sure

### Section B — Voice Assistant Attitudes

**Q4.** If a voice assistant was available in your hotel room — similar to a smart speaker — how comfortable would you be using it to make room service requests?
- [ ] Very comfortable
- [ ] Comfortable
- [ ] Neutral
- [ ] Uncomfortable
- [ ] Very uncomfortable

**Q5.** Would your answer to Q4 change if you knew that all voice processing happened entirely on the device in your room, and no audio or text was ever sent to the internet or recorded?
- [ ] Yes — I would be more comfortable
- [ ] No — I would still be uncomfortable
- [ ] No change in my answer

**Q6.** Which of the following would you be most likely to use a voice assistant for? *(Select all that apply)*
- [ ] Requesting extra towels or bedding
- [ ] Housekeeping / room cleaning
- [ ] Food and beverage orders
- [ ] Wake-up calls
- [ ] Reporting a maintenance issue
- [ ] Requesting toiletries
- [ ] Noise complaints
- [ ] General hotel information (e.g., pool hours, restaurant menu)
- [ ] Checkout or billing questions

### Section C — Confirmation and Privacy

**Q7.** When using a voice assistant to place a room service request, which confirmation method would you prefer?

- [ ] Both — voice readback and an on-screen confirmation
